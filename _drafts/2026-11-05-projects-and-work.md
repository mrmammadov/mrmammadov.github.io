---
title: Projects and work
description: How to fill in your portfolio.
---

Projects live in `_projects/` and each gets its own page. Optional `demo_url` and `repo_url` fields add links under the title.

```yaml
---
title: My project
description: What it does, in one line.
date: 2026-11-01
demo_url: https://example.com
repo_url: https://github.com/username/project
---
```

Work entries live in `_work/`. They don't get their own pages; they're listed on the homepage and on `/work/`, newest first.

```yaml
---
company: Acme
role: Software Engineer
date_start: 2024-01-01
date_end: 2025-06-01   # leave out for "Current"
---
```
